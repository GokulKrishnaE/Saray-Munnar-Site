A 5-Star Resort in Kerala Offering World-Class Amenities

Saray Munnar, a five-star palace resort, is uniquely situated with dense forest on one side and the Western Ghats mountain ranges on the other. This setting, along with the surrounding tea plantations, sets it apart from other resorts in Munnar. Guests can enjoy breathtaking views, an ideal climate, and an untouched landscape perfect for exploring spice plantations, waterfalls, wildlife, tea plantations, and the vibrant life of the local tribal villages. These exceptional amenities and the prime location make Saray Munnar the best five-star deluxe resort in Munnar.

Eagle park

Explore the unique photo opportunities offered by the beautiful park at our resort. The Giant Eagle Park, a marvel created over three years by 300 skilled artisans, stands as the largest park within a resort in Kerala. The impressive giant eagle sculpture has become a beloved selfie spot for travelers. Adorned with wall art crafted by artisans replicating intricate designs reminiscent of Raja forts, the park features caves, waterfalls, adventure zones, expansive fish ponds, and stunning gardens offering panoramic views of the mountains and tea plantations. In the evenings, guests can immerse themselves in the ambiance with campfires and live performances, creating unforgettable memories.
